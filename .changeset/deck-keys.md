Deck keyboard: `PageDown` / `PageUp` move to the next / previous step or slide, so presentation clickers work. Keys pressed with Ctrl, ⌘ or Alt (print, find, back …) and keys in a `<select>` or an editable element are left to the browser instead of also opening the presenter, going fullscreen or changing the slide.

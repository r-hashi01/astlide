Add a pen and a laser pointer. Press `d` (or the `draw` toolbar action) to draw on the slide — `c` clears the current slide's drawing — and `l` (or `laser`) for a laser pointer; `Esc` exits. Drawings are kept per slide while you navigate, and both strokes and the pointer are mirrored between the presenter and audience windows, so you can annotate from the presenter screen. Colors follow `--astlide-pen-color` / `--astlide-laser-color`.

A slide's own markup and styles are framework-agnostic too: `@astlide/core/runtime/slide` renders the `<section class="slide …">` around a slide's content (layout classes, background, PowerPoint template artwork and number) and `@astlide/core/styles/slide.css` styles it (experimental). `Slide.astro` uses both; slides look the same.

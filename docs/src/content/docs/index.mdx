## What sets it apart

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Many decks, one site" icon="list-format">
		Decks are content, not projects: every folder under `src/content/decks/` is a deck, with an index
		page out of the box. Keep a year of talks, a team's reports or a product's training decks in one
		repository — sharing themes, components and one deploy.
	</Card>
	<Card title="An SDK, not an app" icon="puzzle">
		`astlide()` goes into any Astro site — your blog, your Starlight docs, a portal of your own design.
		The pieces work on their own too: the deck loader, PowerPoint import and templates, and the layout
		engine behind the PPTX export.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Your PowerPoint template, editable PPTX out" icon="document">
		Register a `.pptx` / `.potx` template as a theme and Markdown slides come out in its design. Export
		turns them into real PowerPoint text boxes, tables and shapes — laid out without a browser.
	</Card>
	<Card title="PowerPoint & Google Slides in" icon="download">
		Drop a `.pptx` into a deck and its pages become slides, or import it as editable HTML slides — text
		stays text, placeholders keep their layout and master styles.
	</Card>
	<Card title="React, Vue & Svelte inside" icon="seti:react">
		Astro islands: put the charts, demos and design-system components you already have on a slide,
		interactive while you present.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Every slide a real page" icon="seti:html">
		`/my-talk/7` is static HTML — link straight to a slide, load fast, host anywhere, including
		sub-paths like GitHub Pages.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## What people build with it

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="A talk archive" icon="open-book">
		Your own Speaker Deck: every talk at its own URL, searchable, with PDF and PPTX downloads for the
		event's upload form.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Slides next to the docs" icon="seti:markdown">
		Product docs and the decks that explain them in one Starlight site, reusing the same components and
		code samples.
	</Card>
	<Card title="An internal deck portal" icon="laptop">
		Weekly reports, proposals and onboarding decks in the company template, reviewed as pull requests
		and handed out as PowerPoint.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Decks written by agents" icon="rocket">
		Markdown and HTML are what language models write best; Astlide turns them into on-brand, editable
		PowerPoint without a browser in the loop.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## Everything you expect when presenting

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Presenter window" icon="rocket">
		Next-slide preview, steps left, notes and timer. Overview, go-to-slide, pen and laser — mirrored
		to the audience screen.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Reveals & code" icon="seti:typescript">
		`<Fragment>` / `<Fragments>` with `until` and `hide`; Shiki code with `{1|3-5|all}` line steps.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Diagrams & math" icon="random">
		Mermaid diagrams loaded on demand, KaTeX rendered at build time.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Export" icon="download">
		PDF and PNG, editable PowerPoint with your speaker notes, or one-click PDF in the browser.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

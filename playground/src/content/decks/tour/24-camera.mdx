# Camera & recording

<Left>

### <kbd>v</kbd> Camera bubble

Your webcam in a round bubble over the slides. Drag it anywhere — it stays put and keeps playing as you move through the deck.

</Left>
<Right>

### <kbd>r</kbd> Record the talk

Captures the screen or tab with your microphone. Press <kbd>r</kbd> again to stop and download `tour-<date>.webm`.

</Right>

Both need `https://` or `localhost` and your permission.
